About Kristen

Nutrition care can make room for your story, your culture, and what feels possible in everyday life.

Support shaped around the whole person.

Kristen Nyampong, RDN, LD, is a registered dietitian whose approach centers trauma-informed, weight-inclusive nutrition care.

Her work is described as compassionate, culturally grounded, and practical—without shame, judgment, or rigid food rules.
